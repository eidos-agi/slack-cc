# Greenmark Waste Solutions - Company Profile

## Business Summary
Greenmark Waste Solutions is a Dallas-Fort Worth based solid waste collection and recycling services provider, operating as a portfolio company of AIC Holdings.

## Locations & Service Area
- **Primary Office/Yard:** 4316 Langdon Road, Dallas, TX 75241
- **Service Territory:** Greater Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex
- **Parent HQ:** AIC Holdings, Frost Tower Fort Worth, 640 Taylor Street Suite 2400, Fort Worth TX 76102

## Services
### Waste Collection
- **Front-Load Dumpsters:** 2, 4, 6, 8 cubic yard commercial containers
- **Roll-Off Dumpsters:** 20, 30, 40 cubic yard containers for construction/demolition
- **Mini Dumpsters:** Smaller containers for residential/light commercial

### Sanitation
- **Portable Restrooms:** Event and construction site sanitation services

## Target Markets
- Construction contractors & project managers
- Event organizers
- Property managers & apartment complexes
- Commercial businesses (DFW)
