# Waste Management Industry Terms

Vocabulary specific to the waste industry. Greenmark operates commercial waste (NTX) and residential waste (Hometown). These terms appear in conversations, Navusoft data, and operational discussions.

## Service Types

| Term | What it means |
|------|-------------|
| Commercial waste | Business customers — offices, restaurants, retail. Greenmark NTX's primary market. |
| Residential waste | Household curbside pickup. Hometown Disposal's primary market. |
| Roll-off | Large open-top container (10-40 cubic yards) delivered to a site, filled, hauled away. Used for construction, cleanouts. |
| Front-load | Commercial dumpster service. Truck lifts the container from the front. Most common commercial service type. |
| Rear-load | Smaller commercial/residential containers. Truck loads from the rear. |
| Compactor | A machine that compresses waste. Can be stationary (at customer site) or on the truck. |

## Operations

| Term | What it means |
|------|-------------|
| Route | A sequence of stops a driver makes in a day. Route optimization is an NP-hard problem. |
| Lift | One pickup of one container. A key volume metric ("lifts per day"). |
| Haul | One trip to transport waste, usually roll-off. Different from a lift. |
| Service | A scheduled pickup event. Can be weekly, bi-weekly, on-call. |
| Tonnage | Weight of waste collected. Measured at the disposal site (scale house). |
| Disposal | Where waste goes after collection — landfill, transfer station, recycling facility. |
| Disposal cost | What Greenmark pays per ton to dump at the disposal site. A major COGS line item. |
| Transfer station | Intermediate facility where waste is consolidated before going to landfill. |

## Fleet

| Term | What it means |
|------|-------------|
| Truck | The collection vehicle. Types: front-load, rear-load, roll-off, residential automated. |
| Container | The receptacle at the customer site. Dumpsters (2-8 yards), roll-offs (10-40 yards), carts (residential). |
| Cart | Small wheeled bin for residential curbside. Typically 64 or 96 gallon. |
| R&M | Repair & Maintenance. A major fleet cost tracked monthly. |
| Telematics | GPS and sensor data from trucks. 3rd Eye is Greenmark's telematics provider. |
| Driver productivity | Productive hours / total payroll hours. A key efficiency metric. |

## Sales & CRM

| Term | What it means |
|------|-------------|
| Prospect | Potential customer not yet signed. Tracked in HubSpot. |
| Deal pipeline | Stages a prospect moves through before becoming a customer. HubSpot concept. |
| Service agreement | Contract with a customer specifying container size, frequency, and price. |

## People & Roles

| Term | What it means |
|------|-------------|
| Driver | CDL-holding employee who operates collection trucks. |
| Route manager | Oversees a set of routes and drivers. |
| Managed service provider | External IT company. Greenmark uses LB Technologies. |
