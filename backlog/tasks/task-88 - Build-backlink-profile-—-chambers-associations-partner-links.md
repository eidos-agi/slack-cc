## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Backlinks remain one of Google's strongest ranking signals. For local service businesses, the most valuable links come from: chambers of commerce, industry associations, business directories, partner websites, and local community sites.

Current state: Minimal. Yelp + AAGD + a few data aggregators. Near-zero domain authority.

Priority link sources:
- **Chambers:** Dallas Regional Chamber, Fort Worth Chamber, Plano Chamber
- **Industry:** NWRA, SWANA, TXSWANA
- **Directories:** BBB, Angi, HomeAdvisor, Thumbtack
- **Partners:** General contractors, roofing companies, property management companies (reciprocal links)
- **Community:** Sponsor local cleanups (on-brand for waste company), community events

Counterfactual: Every month without building links is a month the domain authority gap with competitors widens.
