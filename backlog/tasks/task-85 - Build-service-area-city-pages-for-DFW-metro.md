## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Create unique landing pages for top DFW cities. This is the #1 organic traffic growth lever for local service businesses. "Dumpster rental Plano TX", "roll-off Fort Worth", "portable restrooms Arlington" are all high-intent searches. Competitors like American AF Dumpsters have individual pages per city per county — we have zero.

Target cities (start with top 8):
- Dallas, Fort Worth, Plano, Arlington, Frisco, Irving, McKinney, Garland

Each page needs 500+ words of UNIQUE content (not template-swapped). Include: local waste management regulations, service availability, specific neighborhoods/areas served, local testimonials if available, and a CTA.

Counterfactual: Without city pages, we lose every geo-modified search to whoever has them.
